## Summary of Honest Status

- **Non-blind / invalid:** C1 (Part 1 Weight Reading), C3 (Part 2 Weight Writing): require re-running in an isolated blind harness (`n >= 20` networks for Part 1).
- **Not replicated:** C6 (MATH-500 improvement `7.9% -> 48.1%` on `Qwen3.5-0.8B` was not replicated; `Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct` showed no degradation at `11.43% -> 11.43%`).
- **Partially replicated:** C5 (Mountain Car rank-1 readout repair in deterministic Python harness, not GPT-6 Astra).
- **Inconclusive (n too small):** C2 (`n=4`), C4 (`n=1` qualitative), E1 (`n=5`), E2 (`n=1` qualitative), E3 (`n=8`).
